Bitcoin Tx Auto-Verifier

Type a txid (or an address to pick from its transactions). It pulls the raw data from two explorers, verifies it with Bitcoin's own math against a fully verified header chain, timestamps the report with OpenTimestamps, and lets you multisig-sign it with PEM keys. Everything runs in your browser.

What is proven, and how

Math, in your browser: raw tx hashes to the txid; merkle proof places it in the block header; header proof-of-work; headers linked to checkpoints from a full verification of all Bitcoin headers since genesis (PoW, links, exact difficulty retargets, timestamp and version rules); block time above the median of the prior 11 blocks.

Cross-checked: raw bytes and status from mempool.space and blockstream.info must match.

Timestamp: the report's SHA-256 goes to OpenTimestamps calendars; the .ots proof anchors into Bitcoin within hours (ots upgrade, then ots verify).

Multisig: ECDSA P-256 / SHA-256 signatures over the exact report bytes; valid only when enough distinct keys from signers.json sign.

Still trusted: the explorer's tip height for confirmation count. Fully trustless means your own node.
