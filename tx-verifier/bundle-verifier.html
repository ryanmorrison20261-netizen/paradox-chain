What this proves — and what it doesn't

Proven by math here: the raw transaction hashes to your txid; that txid is committed in the block header's merkle root; the header's hash meets the proof-of-work target in its own bits field. A forged transaction would have to come with a block someone actually mined.

Main chain: this page carries checkpoints from a full verification of every Bitcoin header from genesis to the anchor tip (proof-of-work, links, exact difficulty retargets, timestamp rules, version rules). A bundle's chain headers must link from the proven block to one of those checkpoints, each with valid PoW, or the check fails.

Still explorer data: the tip height used for the confirmation count, and blocks newer than the anchor tip (refresh the checkpoints to cover them). Fully trustless still means your own node.
